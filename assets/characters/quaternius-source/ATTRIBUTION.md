Selected original glTF, binary geometry and color textures are retained here for reproducibility. Normal/roughness maps are omitted from this lightweight prototype's inputs. Original glTF references to omitted maps are removed by the local Blender conversion script before import.

Derived prototype modifications: transferred/rest-fitted to the project's fixed-length 22-bone rig; finger influences collapsed to wrist for this striker prototype; skin/eyes textures reduced to 512/128 pixels; per-face mint jersey, shorts, socks and boots; in-project idle/runup/kick/recovery animation. Quaternius did not author the added football animation or wardrobe changes. No paid Source assets used.
