# Ten new model, motion and transition rounds

Baseline: published `42f54ba3a48647cd0a529e612b0c6f3fb3d0e6c1`, tree `6fef4db83767ab32b1ac37e051e80ba7c50aa366`. Remote fetched and verified on 2026-10-03. These are ten additional sequential checkpoints; previous rounds are not counted.

## Evidence contract

Each round records its exact source/asset hashes, scope, fresh boundary samples, findings, candidate decision and affected revalidation. Failed candidates remain identified. A passing no-change round is acceptable; repeated aggregate calls are not distinct rounds. Later changes reopen affected earlier checks. Real deformed geometry includes the runtime finger morph. Offline CPU/Blender views are not browser or phone GPU evidence.

The fixed 645-shot / 205-catch manifest remains intact. Palm calibration, held sphere/floor bounds, protected nonadjacent limb crossings and continuity thresholds are not relaxed. Model budgets remain 18,000 triangles / 450,000 bytes, 24 runtime bones and four weights. Heavy jobs run serially with bulky evidence on workspace disk.

## Completed sequential checkpoints

1. **Baseline and anatomy:** reproduce published tests/resource counts and inspect enlarged elbow/cuff/hand silhouettes; pin reference geometry and sources.
2. **Elbow and garment form:** compare bounded offline fairing against the exact baseline, including bent poses and preserved collision surfaces. Accept only an actual visible improvement that passes geometry and asset budgets.
3. **Grip and hand form:** inspect thumb/finger relations and phase boundaries; evaluate any bounded thumb candidate against sphere, floor, self-fold, reset and cost constraints.
4. **Run-up entry:** audit all four techniques at frozen preparation/release, mobile held input, cadence joins and 30/60/120 Hz. Repair only measured visible discontinuities.
5. **Plant to follow-through:** inspect exact ball contact, support drift, target extremes and clip finish, using both recorded captures and any integrated model.
6. **Save to landing:** inspect mirrored low/high saves, arm overlap, body articulation and actual-skin floor/contact through landing.
7. **Recovery to readiness:** evaluate free and held support transfer, hand release, feet and final standing joins, preserving coupled ball/body constraints.
8. **Actual match lifecycle:** exercise repeated/paused/interrupted shot, result, next-round and home transitions with the real main/Stadium harness.
9. **Combined geometry:** run every fixed shot and derived catch uniformly through body/hold/trajectory gates, plus candidate-specific dense windows and affected earlier checks.
10. **Final revision:** full regression/build, matched visual sequence verdict, quiet paired CPU/resource receipts and independent review.
